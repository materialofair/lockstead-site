Start with what you need

Core protection for one selected app is free. VIP adds multi-app management and advanced features. Choose monthly, yearly or lifetime purchase options; subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. Check the App Store for current local prices.

Know what it does

Screen Time authorization is required. Locking restricts app opening; it does not promise to hide icons, suppress notifications or encrypt another app’s files. Background rule changes can be delayed by iOS. Verify protection on a physical device.

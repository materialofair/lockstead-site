Prepare your iPhone for a guest with Lockstead

Guest Mode is intended for lending the same device to someone else. With VIP, choose the apps the guest may use and restrict other apps during the session.

Step by step

  1. Open Lockstead and authenticate with your PIN or configured biometrics.
  2. Choose the apps the guest should be able to use, keeping the list limited to the purpose of the handoff.
  3. Start Guest Mode and inspect its status and duration.
  4. Check both an allowed app and a blocked app before lending the device.
  5. When the session ends, open Lockstead and verify that the expected protection rules have returned.

Before you rely on it

Guest Mode is a same-device workflow, not remote supervision of another phone. Some system behavior is outside an app’s control. Keep the manager PIN private and check protection rather than relying only on a timer.

How to lock selected apps on iPhone with Lockstead

Lockstead uses Apple’s Screen Time technologies to restrict selected apps from opening on the same iPhone or iPad. A six-digit PIN protects access to its settings.

Step by step

  1. Install Lockstead and set a PIN that only the device manager knows.
  2. Grant the requested Screen Time authorization. Without authorization, restrictions cannot be applied.
  3. Choose the app you want to protect. Core protection for one selected app is free.
  4. Enable Lock and confirm the protection status in Lockstead.
  5. Try opening the protected app on the device. Confirm the block appears before handing the device to someone else.

Before you rely on it

An app lock is an access restriction, not encryption of another app’s files. Do not assume it removes the app’s icon or hides notifications. The restrictions depend on current system authorization and device behavior.

Temporary access and schedules: what to check

Temporary access provides a period in which protected apps may be opened. Daily schedules can apply protection during a chosen window. Check the current rule and the actual device behavior at each transition.

Step by step

  1. Review the apps currently selected and whether protection is active.
  2. Start temporary access for the time needed, or configure the daily protection window.
  3. Confirm that an app expected to open is available during the allowed period.
  4. At the end of the period, verify that the expected restriction has returned.
  5. If behavior differs, open Lockstead, check Screen Time authorization and inspect the active settings before relying on the rule.

Before you rely on it

iOS controls background scheduling and may delay a rule change. Do not treat expiry as a guaranteed second-by-second boundary. This guide describes currently published core workflows, not unreleased multi-window scheduling features.

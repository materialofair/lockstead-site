## English

### Product
Lockstead — App Store ID 6791830193.

### Purpose
On-device app access control for the iPhone or iPad you lend to a child, friend or family member.

### Platform
iPhone and iPad, iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later, with Screen Time authorization.

### Core controls
Selected-app blocking, six-digit PIN, temporary access and daily schedules.

### VIP
Multi-app management, advanced rules, Guest Mode, app-removal controls and personalization. Monthly, yearly and lifetime options are offered.

### Privacy
No app account or developer backend. Protection settings remain local; purchases use Apple services.

### Limits
Not a remote management service. Does not guarantee notification suppression or icon hiding. iOS may delay background changes.

### Public resources
The public lockstead-site repository hosts product and support information. The app source is private.

Screen Time authorization is required. Locking restricts app opening; it does not promise to hide icons, suppress notifications or encrypt another app’s files. Background rule changes can be delayed by iOS. Verify protection on a physical device.

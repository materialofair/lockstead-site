Lockstead Privacy Policy

Effective: July 14, 2026

Our promise is simple: Lockstead collects nothing.

Data we collect

None. Lockstead has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Where your settings live

Your lock configuration, PIN (stored as a salted hash), schedule, and preferences are stored only on your device, in the app's private App Group container. They are removed when you delete the app.

Screen Time authorization

Lockstead uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to lock and hide apps. The authorization and the list of restricted apps are managed by iOS on your device. Apple's opaque tokens prevent Lockstead from knowing which apps you selected by name, and nothing is transmitted anywhere. You can revoke this authorization at any time in Settings > Screen Time.

Face ID / Touch ID

If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is performed entirely by iOS. Lockstead never sees or stores your biometric data.

Purchases

VIP purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Lockstead only receives Apple's confirmation that a purchase is active — no payment details, no identity.

Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.

Lockstead 隐私政策

生效日期:2026 年 7 月 14 日

我们的承诺很简单:Lockstead 不收集任何数据。

我们收集什么

无。Lockstead 没有账号体系、没有服务器、没有统计分析、没有第三方 SDK,应用自身不发起任何网络请求。

你的设置存放在哪里

锁定配置、PIN(以加盐哈希存储)、时间表与偏好设置仅保存在你的设备上(应用的私有 App Group 容器内),删除应用即被移除。

屏幕使用时间授权

Lockstead 使用 Apple 屏幕使用时间框架(Family Controls)锁定与隐藏 App。授权与受限 App 列表由 iOS 在你的设备上管理;Apple 的不透明令牌机制使 Lockstead 无法得知你选择的 App 名称,任何信息都不会被传输。你可随时在「设置 > 屏幕使用时间」中撤销授权。

面容 ID / 触控 ID

若开启生物识别解锁,认证完全由 iOS 完成,Lockstead 无法访问或存储你的生物特征数据。

购买

VIP 购买与订阅由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。Lockstead 仅收到 Apple 的购买生效确认——不涉及任何支付信息或身份信息。

变更与联系

若本政策发生变更,新版本将发布于本页面。如有疑问,请在 GitHub 提交 issue。

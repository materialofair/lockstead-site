Contact / 联系我们

Open a support issue on GitHub ↗

Include your device model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce the issue. GitHub issues are public: do not attach private photos, passwords, personal documents or purchase receipts.

请提供设备型号、iOS 版本、应用版本与复现步骤。GitHub 问题是公开的，请勿上传私人照片、密码、个人文档或购买凭证。

Purchases / 购买

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Apple handles billing and refund requests. / 使用原购买 Apple 账号恢复购买；账单和退款由 Apple 处理。

Before removing the app / 卸载之前

Screen Time authorization is required. Locking restricts app opening; it does not promise to hide icons, suppress notifications or encrypt another app’s files. Background rule changes can be delayed by iOS. Verify protection on a physical device.

需要屏幕使用时间授权。锁定限制应用打开，不保证隐藏图标、抑制通知或加密其他应用文件。iOS 可能延迟后台规则切换，请在真机核对保护效果。
